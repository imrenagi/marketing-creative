# Motion Art Director

Read the marketing brief, selected creative brief, brand material and requested placements. Preserve the selected persuasive idea; alternative campaign concepts belong to creative-ideation.

## Derive the look

Use [styles.md](references/styles.md), [motion-language.md](references/motion-language.md), [explainers.md](references/explainers.md) and [visual-examples.md](references/visual-examples.md) only as relevant. For a new open visual direction, show a small set of alternatives; for a selected look, develop it without reopening selection.

Write `STYLE.md`: desired feeling; palette with source/reason for each color; display and supporting type with font files/availability; imagery and object-render family; typography hierarchy; background surface/motion; camera and transition logic; one special moment when useful; captions; per-ratio layouts. Names such as “cinematic” are insufficient without these decisions.

The [concept library](references/concepts.md) is shared with ideation. Its examples are construction principles, not mandatory scene orders. Keep the user's requested style; a starter's placeholder colors or previous project's palette are not brand evidence.

For a nontrivial HyperFrames treatment, use hyperframes-creative's house-style, video-composition and typography guidance, then hyperframes-animation for the chosen motion language. Search hyperframes-registry before hand-building a named effect or scene primitive; use media-use for source-aware photographic polish and frozen assets. The [capability map](../motion-video-producer/references/hyperframes-routing.md) explains availability and boundaries; preserve the selected brand and intentional stillness.

## Compose for meaning

Keep a clear focal point. Move the camera to follow, reveal or compare; object action may carry a shot with a stationary camera. Continuous worlds, character stages, purposeful cuts, designed stillness and photo collage are all valid. Do not force perpetual movement, a fixed scene count or photoreal imagery.

Use actual captures for evidence and faithful demonstrations. Product-grounded simplified DOM/vector UI is appropriate for explanatory animation; identify reconstruction in production notes and do not invent features, data or outcomes. Label fictional construction examples. Generated scenes must not masquerade as documentary evidence.

Read [placement profiles](../motion-video-producer/references/placements.md). Design 16:9, 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 according to requested outputs. Side-by-side landscape can become stacked portrait; adjust crop, line length, camera, captions and asset detail. Do not shrink the entire landscape frame or assume a central crop works.

## Handoff

Pass `STYLE.md` and asset requirements to storyboard/composer. Use existing assets first and available media tools for sourcing/generation when authorized. Record provenance and dimensions in the project asset manifest. Font and media licenses/credits travel with deliverables where required. No provider is mandatory for visual direction.
