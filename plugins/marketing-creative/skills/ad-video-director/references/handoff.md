# A brief someone can execute

Scale the deliverable to the request. A short revision needs less material than a campaign, but exact words and actions still matter.

## Brief

Record goal/metric; language/market/placement; audience situation and awareness; barrier/belief change; one promise; mechanism; proof; real offer/destination; duration; tone; format; resources; constraints; assumptions; facts awaiting verification.

Separate the creative hypothesis from execution. A low-cost version should preserve persuasion, not merely imitate an expensive look.

## Script contract

| Time | Persuasive job | Exact voice/dialogue | Exact overlay | Shot/action, asset/proof | Edit/audio cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.0 | Relevant friction | Ready-to-say line or silence | Literal visible text | First frame and action | Only a meaningful cue |

Cover all time once. Mark simultaneous events, continuous shots, silence and holds. A one-second breakdown is an execution view of one script, not unrelated ideas.

For capture: task, start state, interaction, useful result, readable crop. For people: setting, delivery, useful gestures, evidence B-roll. For animation: motif and what transitions communicate. Use actual paths for existing assets, and descriptive requirements for missing assets rather than fictitious filenames.

## Placement and sound

For vertical feeds, 9:16 is a useful starting master. Verify placement specs and UI before production/upload. Center focal content, leave room for captions/buttons/right rail, and inspect placement previews. The deck's approximately 15% top and 30–35% bottom exclusions are sketching guidance, not universal current measurements. Centering does not guarantee a successful 4:5 crop; inspect and reframe each export.

Make sound-on viewing intelligible and core meaning understandable without sound through captions, action, and attribution. Check music, creator, and footage rights for intended paid usage. Organic permission or a library track does not automatically grant paid cross-platform rights.

## Production notes

Include decisions needed to shoot: captures, talent direction, hierarchy, brand cues, subtitles, sound, safe-zone preview, ownership, claim checks, destination alignment, exports. Identify where edits help and where a continuous take proves the task.

Supply placement copy when it matters: primary text, headline, CTA/button suggestion, cover. Avoid invented character limits; make the first visible caption useful without "see more."

For actual production, hand the script, runtime, aspect ratio, assets, factual constraints, and authorization state to the available workflow. This skill requires no renderer, API, account, paid tool, or other skill. Never claim an MP4 exists when the deliverable is a script.
