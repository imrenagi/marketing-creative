# Research basis and source limits

Researched 12 October 2026. This is a synthesis for creative decisions, not a claim that a universal ad formula has been experimentally proven. Source-reported results remain their publishers' claims. Most case studies combine creative, targeting, placement, spend and optimization. Do not attribute campaign results to a hook or editing pattern without an appropriate experiment.

## How to use the evidence

Distinguish platform guidance, published campaign descriptions, directly inspected creative, illustrative teaching models, and original planning heuristics. The public campaign examples below were researched through published source text, not independently frame-analyzed videos. The two local user videos were inspected separately; their notes are deliberately not distributed in this plugin. No sample ROAS, CPA or incremental sales data was supplied. TikTok's public Top Ads page returned no creative inventory in this research session; no unseen ads or performance ranks are claimed.

## Platform principles

1. [TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes): platform-native execution, early relevance, a hook/body/close argument, purposeful stimulation, sound, and vertical framing with UI room. The [accompanying creative article](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads) emphasizes the opening six seconds. Use this as support for getting to value quickly, not a universal three-second causal threshold or instruction to cut constantly.
2. [Google Ads: ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en): attention, brand attribution, human connection, direction; objective-specific priorities and Shorts guidance. Supports choosing emphasis by awareness/consideration/action, rather than applying one order blindly. This skill's detailed timing maps are editorial synthesis, not Google's prescribed schedules.
3. [Meta: Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads): vertical video, sound and key messages inside UI-safe areas. Indexed official content was available; opening the page redirected to login. Use the directional principle and verify current placement previews/specs before exporting. Do not reproduce a headline average as this product's expected lift.
4. [TikTok Creative Center overview](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-center?lang=en-GB) and [Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/mobile/en): useful discovery and segment-level diagnostics, with advertiser authorization/selection limits. Public examples are a selected set, not a census of all winners; a rank or view count does not prove profitability.

## Eight campaign examples across products and objectives

### 1. InDrive — localized emotion, mechanism, and changing CTA

[Google / InDrive campaign account](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/future-of-marketing/creative-advertising-video-ads-taxi-app/), published 2023. The campaign begins with relatable ride-price frustration, links the resolution to agreeing a price in the app, and localizes language, currency, and setting. The source reports 120 million reach and 5.6% brand-awareness lift across its first phase. Transfer: connect emotional tension to the product mechanism and adapt the ask to market familiarity. Limits: multi-country rebrand results do not isolate one script or establish acquisition economics.

### 2. Goodcall — creator explanation for a B2B product

[TikTok / Goodcall](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/inspiration/goodcall-drives-signups-with-spark-ads). The source describes creator partnerships and conversion campaigns for AI phone software serving small businesses, with organic content amplified through Spark Ads. It reports customer acquisition cost falling from $185 to $7 per signup and over 6,000 accounts. Transfer: make an abstract tool understandable through a credible person's relevant use case. Limits: signup cost is not retained-customer economics; the combined campaign does not isolate creator style or any opening line.

### 3. Canva — sound as a test variable

[TikTok / Canva](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-AU/inspiration/canva-commercial-music-library). The campaign compared original sound with a commercially licensed trending track while communicating design ease through creator content. The publisher reports a week-on-week 100% increase in new active users and 80% reduction in cost per new activation. Transfer: specify audio deliberately and test it separately from the argument. Limits: the article describes an A/B test but the reported week-on-week summaries are not a complete experimental readout; no confidence intervals or full allocation details are provided. Verify licensing for every destination platform.

### 4. La Rosée — different creative jobs within one funnel

[TikTok sunscreen case-study PDF](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/LaRosee.pdf), one page. Upper-funnel executions used native trends; lower-funnel executions focused on product how-to and offers. Transfer: discovery and purchase ads need different evidence and sequencing. The document reports sales/ROAS improvements, but headline and body figures for views/sales use inconsistent labels or scopes. Do not use its numbers as a clean causal benchmark. Its textual creative distinction, rather than numeric precision, informs this skill.

### 5. e.l.f. Soft Glam Concealer — creator assets and launch reach

[TikTok / e.l.f. Branded Buzz](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/elf-cosmetics-branded-buzz). Creator content was amplified through Spark Ads alongside premium launch/search placements. The source reports 143 million impressions and lifts in awareness and recall. Transfer: preserve a useful creator point of view while making product identity and demonstration legible; separate launch awareness from conversion evidence. Limits: a bundled media strategy cannot prove that native appearance, one hook, or a specific edit caused sellout.

### 6. Duolingo — entertainment linked to product logic

[TikTok / Duolingo Ad Awards](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/ad-awards-duolingo-case-study), 2025 campaign. Serialized mascot lore incorporated app mechanics and was amplified through Spark Ads. The publisher reports 34.8 million impressions, 26.9 million reach and 12.63% engagement. Transfer: distinctive characters can turn product logic into memorable entertainment when audiences understand the context. Limits: engagement and cultural conversation do not establish paid subscriptions or retained learners. An unfamiliar startup cannot assume the same pre-existing brand recognition.

### 7. Creative Converters — product desire, offer, and destination

[TikTok / Creative Converters](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/creative-converters). A fashion relaunch combined unconventional product creatives, offers, web-conversion optimization, and catalog shopping ads. The source reports 6.4× ROAS and more than 154,000 completed orders over roughly six weeks. Transfer: show why the product is desirable and align the action with a relevant product destination. Limits: aggregate attributed ROAS depends on media, offer, targeting and attribution; it does not validate a particular pacing recipe.

### 8. Chatbooks — a buyer's life supplies the story

[Harmon Brothers' Chatbooks campaign release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/harmon-brothers-follow-up-digital-scrapbooking-campaign-for-chatbooks-is-a-wild-ride-300683245.html), 2018. The agency describes family-vacation comedy demonstrating a parent-friendly photo-book product. Transfer: a recognizable buyer situation can carry an extended product explanation when each comic event remains related to the product's job. Limits: this is an agency promotional release; reported views and agency-wide sales are not independent performance evidence for the sequel. Do not import its claims or long runtime without need.

## Synthesis and research gaps

The reusable principle is a chain: relevant attention → understandable product mechanism → credible payoff → appropriate next step. Brand attribution runs through that chain. No reviewed evidence establishes a universal optimal length, mandatory one-second cut frequency, exact payoff percentage, or guaranteed performance from a hook formula.

Useful follow-up research for a specific brief: current ads from the same category and market; authentic customer objections; the product's current first-use flow and offer; comparable campaign-level results with downstream quality. Directly inspect available videos rather than guessing a transcript from a thumbnail. Keep selection bias, rights, and missing performance context visible.
