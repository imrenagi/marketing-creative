# Make creative learning testable

Define the changed element, comparable elements, primary outcome, diagnostics, actual observation window/volume where available, and what the result could change. Do not invent budget, sample threshold, or statistical confidence.

Explore in stages when appropriate: different buyer arguments first; modular hook/demo/proof/CTA changes within a promising concept next; creator/shot/text/pacing/sound execution after that. Concept exploration does not isolate one cause.

A first batch might be three hooks sharing body, proof, offer, destination, and CTA. Three hooks with three different bodies are nine concepts, not a clean hook comparison. Fit the batch to resources.

| Signal | Possible causes | Next investigation / test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early drop | Relevance, slow opening, unclear frame, delivery, placement/audience mismatch | Distinct first-frame/line hypotheses with comparable body |
| Drop in setup | Repetition, weak stakes, delayed value, reading/speech overload | Shorter setup or earlier result |
| Drop in demo | Tiny UI, too much text, irrelevant task, slow action | Crop useful action and clarify outcome |
| Attention without action | Entertainment mismatch, attribution/proof, audience, offer, unclear ask | Inspect argument, audience, destination, tracking before choosing a bottleneck |
| Clicks without conversion | Message/offer mismatch, page speed/usability, terms, tracking, low intent | Inspect post-click funnel before blaming CTA |
| Installs without activation | Promise differs from first-use reality, setup friction, user quality | Align ad with actual first useful action |
| Decline over time | Saturation, frequency, auction, seasonality, tracking/product change, fatigue | Compare like-for-like periods before declaring fatigue |

Retention curves diagnose; they do not prove causation. A cumulative "still watching" curve normally does not rise from a re-hook. Examine slope, segment retention, replays, and actual platform definitions. Low CTR alone cannot diagnose a weak CTA; completion cannot establish sales potential.

Record denominators, platform definitions, attribution windows, objective, dates, spend/volume if given, and experimental versus observational evidence. Different view/hook rates are not automatically comparable. Sparse or uneven delivery can be inconclusive. Keep offer, audience, page, and campaign changes visible.

Select winners against the goal and downstream quality. An attention-winning hook can produce no qualified actions. For causal questions, use a suitable experiment/holdout when available; ordinary delivery does not create equal comparable groups.

Organic posts can reveal language, comments, shares, and retention cheaply. Paid audiences and optimization differ: organic success is a candidate signal, neither prerequisite nor proof of paid profitability. Amplify an existing authorized post when preserving social context helps, after checking rights and mechanics. Planning never implies spending or publishing.
