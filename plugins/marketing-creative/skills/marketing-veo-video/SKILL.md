# Marketing Veo Video

Generate a standalone marketing asset or a clip for [motion-video-producer](../motion-video-producer/SKILL.md), which owns the finished timeline and mixed-media assembly. This skill is opt-in because provider generation adds cost. A general request to produce an ad or invoking this skill to inspect/plan is not approval to spend.

## Prepare, then approve

Read [capabilities.md](references/capabilities.md) for the current model/mode limits and [workflow.md](references/workflow.md) for configuration and commands. View supplied media, preserve the brief's claims and chosen concept, and write a prompt describing action, framing, camera, light, continuity and intended audio. Avoid baking critical marketing copy into footage when deterministic typography is needed.

Use [veo_video.py](scripts/veo_video.py) submit --dry-run. Present the actual prompt/media, model, mode, clip count, duration, ratio, resolution and current cost estimate, including possible input/extension charges. Obtain explicit user approval for that prepared request before passing its requestSha256 as --approve-request with a factual --approval-note. The helper refuses unpaid planning authorization, missing approval or a changed request fingerprint. Every new generation, extension or retry needs approval covering that request; status and download resume existing work.

## Generate and recover

The helper reads exported GEMINI_API_KEY using the README's external credential convention. It never loads .env, retries a submission automatically, switches models or overwrites an existing run. A timeout can leave the outcome unknown: preserve state and investigate the provider operation before a new paid request. Poll status once per command and return to other work; download promptly within provider retention.

## Review and handoff

Inspect downloaded footage and listen to its native soundtrack. Check product/character fidelity, claims, unexpected text, continuity and actual measured dimensions/duration/audio. A filtered response or HTTP success is not a finished asset. Preserve generated bytes when checks fail; resolve editorial issues deliberately.

Deliver standalone clips with assets.json and review notes, or hand the local file, hash, provenance, measured media metadata and intended audio policy to motion-video-producer. Veo's native ratios are 16:9 and 9:16; the producer composes independent 4:5 and 1:1 deliverables with framing review. Generated scenes are illustrative, not evidence of actual product behavior or customer results.
