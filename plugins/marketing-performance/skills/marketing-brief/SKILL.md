# Marketing Brief

Establish what the campaign must accomplish and what it can truthfully communicate. Read supplied material and existing project artifacts before asking for missing facts. A brief supports several creative ideas; do not select a hook, style, or scene sequence here.

## Build the strategic foundation

Use [brief-contract.md](references/brief-contract.md). Establish the objective and primary outcome metric; audience, market and language; awareness, buying situation and barriers; positioning, supported value propositions and differentiators; mechanism and proof; actual offer, terms, CTA and destination; channels, placements, formats, durations and resources.

Separate supplied facts, source-supported evidence, provisional hypotheses and unknowns. Never invent prices, trials, customer statements, deadlines, product screens or results. If information is missing, draft with explicit assumptions where possible and ask only what changes the decision. Keep unsupported claims out of launch-ready copy.

An install campaign and an activation campaign need different success criteria. Do not infer sales performance from attention or engagement alone. Choose evidence that addresses the audience’s actual barrier; record when a proxy metric cannot establish the business outcome.

## Define deliverables early

Record actual placement names, not just “desktop” or “phone.” Verify current official requirements for selected placements and record source/date; otherwise mark upload limits and safe areas unverified. Support 16:9, 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1; select only the variants requested or needed for the selected placements. Production sizes are presets, not universal upload requirements.

## Deliver and hand off

Write `MARKETING_BRIEF.md` in the user's chosen project when saving is requested or part of authorized production; otherwise deliver the brief in chat. Include evidence and open decisions. Reuse an existing campaign directory; when working with `campaign-utm`, save the brief alongside its `campaign.json` and preserve its tracking records. For alternative approaches, hand the saved brief to `$creative-ideation` from the optional marketing-creative plugin when available, or provide it as the creative handoff. This plugin works without creative tooling. Do not generate a full video script or start production unless requested.

A strategic revision updates the brief and identifies which concept, script or placement assumptions need reconsideration. Preserve unrelated approved decisions.
